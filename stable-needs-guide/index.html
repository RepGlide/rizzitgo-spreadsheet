Stable Needs Guide

Keep category, use, necessary options, and open conditions in view before you browse.

Needs outlast signals

Popularity language changes fast; your need does not. Hold the need steady before, during, and after you browse, so a label never replaces the reason you are looking.

Category

Fix the category first. An item that is trending in the wrong category is still the wrong category.

Use

State what the item is for. The use is the anchor every other field is checked against.

Necessary options

List the color, size, version, material, and quantity you actually need. These are stable requirements, not signals.

Open conditions

Keep whatever still needs confirmation as an open condition. Do not let a hot label close a question for you.

✓Confirmation boundary: the need stays fixed; only the field you confirm on the page counts as met.
Beat noteIf popularity language is replacing the need, stop and return to category, use, and options.

A worked hold

  1. Category: a crossbody bag.
  2. Use: daily carry.
  3. Options: black, medium, single item.
  4. Open condition: the material is unstated.
  5. Result: the need stays fixed while the label is set aside.

Teaching composition — not trend data, verification, or a saved preference.
